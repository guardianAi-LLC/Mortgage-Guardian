## 4. Description

Mortgage servicing errors affect millions of homeowners every year -- from miscalculated escrow accounts to misapplied payments. Most people never realize they are being overcharged. Mortgage Guardian puts the power of AI-driven analysis in your hands so you can catch these costly mistakes before they compound.

**Key Features:**

- **Document Scanning & OCR** -- Snap a photo or upload mortgage statements, escrow analyses, payment histories, and closing documents. Our advanced OCR engine extracts every detail automatically.

- **AI-Powered Analysis** -- Claude AI reviews your documents against RESPA regulations, TILA requirements, and standard mortgage servicing practices to identify errors, overcharges, and compliance violations.

- **Bank Account Verification** -- Securely connect your bank through Plaid to cross-reference mortgage payments with your actual transaction history. Detect misapplied payments, duplicate charges, and timing discrepancies instantly.

- **RESPA-Compliant Dispute Letters** -- When issues are found, generate professionally formatted Notice of Error and Request for Information letters ready to send to your servicer, complete with legal citations and supporting evidence.

- **Comprehensive Audit Dashboard** -- Track all identified issues by severity, monitor potential savings, and manage your dispute timeline from a single screen.

**How It Works:**

1. Upload your mortgage documents by taking a photo or importing from your files.
2. Mortgage Guardian analyzes each document using AI, flagging errors and calculating potential overcharges.
3. Review findings, generate dispute letters, and take action to recover what you are owed.

**Your Privacy Matters:**

All document processing uses end-to-end encryption. Biometric authentication protects your data on-device. Bank connections are powered by Plaid's bank-level security infrastructure. We never sell or share your personal information.
