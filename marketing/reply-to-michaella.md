## What This Email Accomplishes

- **Zero anchoring** — no valuation number, no disclosure of financials, no admission of urgency to sell.
- **Scarcity signal** — "we're not actively marketing" implies you have options and aren't desperate.
- **Filter for legitimacy** — real PE principals answer all four questions without hesitation; brokers, scammers, and tire-kickers evaporate.
- **NDA gate** — nothing proprietary leaves your hands until she signs.
- **Sets the power dynamic** — you are qualifying *her*, not the reverse.

## Red Flags to Watch For in Her Reply

| If she says... | It likely means... | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| "I can't disclose the fund name until we have a call" | Broker or tire-kicker | Decline until she names the firm |
| "We're representing multiple funds looking at this space" | She's an M&A advisor, not a principal | Ask who pays her fee — buyer or seller |
| "We only need 15 minutes to discuss high-level" | Fishing for info, not a real deal | Require NDA first |
| Names a real, verifiable PE firm + thesis + check size | Legitimate inquiry | Proceed with NDA, then intro call |
| Asks for your revenue / financials before NDA | Standard early screen OR data harvesting | Share only public info until NDA signed |
