## Why This Email Works

- **You're not asking permission** — you're setting the terms of the call.
- **Three legitimate pre-call questions** that any real PE principal will answer in 60 seconds.
- **NDA mention** — frames you as a professional, not a retail founder who can be walked over.

## What to Watch For in Her Reply

- **Names the firm + sends a thesis** → Legit. Research them immediately. Green light.
- **Sends firm name but dodges thesis** → Possibly a cold-sweep associate. Proceed cautiously.
- **"Let's just discuss on the call"** → Red flag. She's either a broker or fishing. Still take the call, but share nothing proprietary.
- **Goes silent until Friday** → She was never serious. Downgrade your time investment.
- **Pushes back on NDA** → Hard red flag. No legitimate acquirer refuses a mutual NDA.

5. **Google the firm name + "lawsuit" OR "SEC action" OR "complaint"** → 5 minutes of due diligence has saved founders millions.
